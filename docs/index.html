EDEN is a verification and resource-intelligence layer that finds reusable work, skips unnecessary recomputation, and seals cryptographic evidence of what ran, what was reused, and what was avoided.

Declared architect: Daniel Noel Clarke · Warwick, UK · v1.0.0-reference

01 What EDEN does

Verified reuse

Exact request commitments. Hits only after binding checks—not a silent cache.

Keep / void

Classifies what stays useful versus what can be dropped without breaking reconstruction.

Provenance

Marbles and receipts bind input, output, policy, and measurements for audit.

Sits underneath

Designed to run beneath models, apps, and pipelines—without replacing them.
